# .impeccable.md — Paw Registry Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary:** Dog breeders — responsible, detail-oriented people who take their craft seriously. They manage waiting lists, health records, litters, and client relationships. They use the admin back-office daily and need it to be dense but not chaotic.

**Secondary:** Prospective puppy clients — emotionally invested, often anxious, looking for reassurance that they've chosen a trustworthy breeder. They access the client portal to track their place in the waiting list and receive updates. The experience should feel warm and personal, not transactional.

**Job to be done:** For breeders — run their operation efficiently without switching between tools. For clients — feel informed, cared for, and confident in their breeder.

**Emotional goal:** Clients should feel calm and trusted. Breeders should feel in control and professional.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Trustworthy, warm, quality.

**Voice:** Caring but competent. Like a small business owner who takes real pride in their work — not clinical, not corporate, not cutesy. Think handwritten notes on quality stationery.

**Emotional register:**
- Public site: welcoming, reassuring, aspirational ("Raised with love. Placed with care.")
- Client portal: personal, clear, supportive
- Admin: efficient, no-nonsense, but still warm in tone

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Organic warmth with quiet refinement. Earthy neutrals, generous whitespace, serif headings that feel considered. Like a well-designed artisan shop — not a startup dashboard.

**Primary reference:** [Good Dog](https://www.gooddog.com/)
- Warm cream/beige backgrounds (not stark white)
- Image-forward gallery cards with generous spacing
- Smooth, unhurried hover transitions
- Dark navy/charcoal for trust anchors, not cold gray
- Conversational copy, human-centered imagery

**Anti-references:**
- Not hyper-modern or brutalist (no sharp angles, no heavy drop shadows, no flashy gradients)
- Not corporate SaaS (no dark mode dashboards, no dense data tables as the primary pattern)
- Not pet-store cutesy (no cartoon paw prints everywhere, no overuse of primary colors)

**Theme:** Light mode only, permanently. Cream/warm white backgrounds, not pure white. The stone palette already in use is directionally correct — lean into it.

**Accent color:** Warm terracotta/amber (`brand-500: #c47420`) for primary actions. Use sparingly as a trust signal, not decoration.

---

### Design Principles

1. **Warmth over efficiency.** When in doubt, add breathing room. Generous padding, soft borders, and whitespace communicate care — cramped layouts communicate stress.

2. **Image-forward.** Dogs and puppies are the product. Photography should be prominent and gallery-style wherever possible. Cards should lead with the image, not metadata.

3. **Quiet confidence.** No flashy animations, no heavy shadows, no bold gradients. Transitions should be smooth and subtle (200–300ms ease). The design should feel settled, not excitable.

4. **Readable hierarchy.** Serif headings (Merriweather) for titles, sans-serif (Inter) for body and UI. Don't mix weight and style arbitrarily — let the type system do the work.

5. **Consistent status language.** Status badges are domain-critical. Color-coding (green, amber, blue, purple, red) must stay consistent across all views. Never introduce new semantic colors without updating all badge variants.

---

### Technical Design Notes

- **Tailwind CSS v4** — CSS-first, no config file. All design tokens live in `client/src/index.css` under `@theme`.
- **No external icon library** — uses emoji for visual indicators. Keep this intentional and consistent.
- **Three distinct zones, one palette:**
  - Public: light stone backgrounds, brand accents, serif hero headings
  - Portal: white cards on stone-50, functional and calm
  - Admin: dark stone-900 sidebar, white content area — the one exception to the warm-light rule
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first with `md:` breakpoint. Off-canvas sidebars on mobile.
- **Gallery pattern** (inspired by Good Dog): image-forward cards in responsive grids (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3`), with subtle hover elevation (`hover:shadow-md transition-shadow`).
